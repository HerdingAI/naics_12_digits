## Quality Metrics

### Extension Guidelines Being Followed:
- ✅ 5-10 sub-categories per 6-digit code (target)
- ✅ Clear differentiation criteria
- ✅ Mutually exclusive categories
- ✅ Business-relevant distinctions
- ✅ Descriptive naming conventions

### Differentiation Criteria Used:
- Product Type
- Service Type
- Customer Segment (B2B, B2C, B2G)
- Business Model
- Production Method
- Technology/Scale
- Market Segment
- End Use
- Specialty/Focus Area
